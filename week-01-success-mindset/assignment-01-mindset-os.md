## Task 2: Three Objective Truths Discovered Through Experimentation

Write three objective truths you discovered through your own actions and results. For each truth, include one sentence for the truth and two to four lines of evidence from your life.

### Truth #1: Results often come from consistency, not from the first attempts.

**Evidence from My Life**

When I started posting on LinkedIn, I hardly got any reactions or engagement. Sometimes it felt like nobody was paying attention, and it made me question whether I should continue. But I started noticing that people who kept sharing their work and showing up consistently were gradually getting noticed. It made me realise that just because you aren't seeing results now doesn't mean your efforts are going to waste.

### Truth #2: Skills grow faster through practice than through information alone.

**Evidence from My Life**

I used to think that watching tutorials and reading about technical concepts was enough. But I realised that I understood things much better when I started working through practical tasks, making mistakes and figuring out how to solve them.

### Truth #3: Growth feels uncomfortable before it feels rewarding.

**Evidence from My Life**

Right now, I'm pushing myself to do things that I wouldn't normally feel comfortable doing. I'm learning new skills, sharing my journey on LinkedIn and putting myself out there. Sometimes it feels awkward, and I still have moments of uncertainty, but I'm learning to embrace those experiences instead of avoiding them.
